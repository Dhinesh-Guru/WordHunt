This is an Multiplayer/single player game.
The user can play with AI(single player mode) or with other user/users with two or more devices(Multiplayer mode).
In multiplayer mode , the users can invite each other to play the game.
The host will generate a game id and share it with the other users.
The other users will join the game using the game id.
In player mode each user has to play/join the game by their own individual device. The players are open the game by opening the localhost link on their device and login with their own id, password then join the game with the game id & password created by the host.

Order for players to guess the letter:

    ->First player is the user who entered the game first and he/she will be in the first position to guess the letter or word.
    ->Then the next player who enter after him play their turn one by one.
    ->After the last player guess the letter or word then the first player will get their second turn to guess the letter or word.
    ->This cycle will continue until the end of the game.
    ->A player has only 30 seconds for a turn to guess the word he has to guess a word before 30 seconds. if he doesn't guess any word by click the tick button after put something on the guess input box before 30 seconds the user will lost their turn, the next user will guess the word he has to wait for the next turn. 
    -> The 30 seconds was being refreshed for every player in every turn. If user_1 guess the word at 15 seconds remaining(it's right or wrong that's not a matter) , the user_2 will have the timer of 30 seconds to guess the letter for their turn. And at the end of the last user the user_1 will get again 30 seconds to guess the letter for his second turn.
